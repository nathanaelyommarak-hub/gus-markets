What is Gus Markets?

Gus Markets is a YouTube Shorts channel that publishes short, daily finance news videos: pre-market and closing recaps, crypto updates, the chart of the day, macro calendar highlights and quick explainers. Every Short is produced by an automated pipeline from public market data and news headlines.

Find the channel at youtube.com/@GusMarkets.
