Privacy Policy

Gus Markets Uploader · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the “Gus Markets Uploader” application (the “App”) handles information. The App is operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Gus Markets (@GusMarkets) and is used solely by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on the owner’s own YouTube channel.

Who uses the App

The App is not offered to the public. The only Google account that authorizes the App is the channel owner’s own account. No other users sign in to or use the App.

Google OAuth scopes and how they are used

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube – to manage the owner’s own YouTube channel, for example updating the channel description and the metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, playlists) of the channel’s own videos.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload the channel’s own videos (Shorts and the daily long-form update) and their thumbnails to the owner’s own YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl – to manage captions (subtitle files) of the channel’s own videos and to read, post, pin or moderate comments on the channel’s own videos.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – to read aggregated performance statistics of the owner’s own channel and videos (for example views, watch time, retention). These statistics are used only to improve the channel’s content and are never shared with third parties.

The App requests no other scopes and uses these scopes only for the purposes listed above.

Data storage and security

The OAuth refresh token for the owner’s account is stored only as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in the owner’s repository and is used solely by the automated upload workflow. It is never written to the public repository, logs, or any other location. The App does not operate a database of user data.

Data we collect

The App processes only data belonging to or appearing on the owner’s own channel: channel and video metadata, captions, aggregated (non-personal) YouTube Analytics statistics, and public comments posted on the channel’s own videos, which are read solely to reply to or moderate them. The App does not build viewer profiles, does not collect contact details of viewers, and does not access any other channel’s data.

Sharing and selling

No data obtained through Google APIs is shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party. No data is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy – Limited Use

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the App’s single purpose (uploading and managing videos on the owner’s own YouTube channel); it is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide that purpose, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; it is not used for serving advertisements; and it is not read by humans except with the owner’s consent, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or for internal operations of the App.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored refresh token stops working and is deleted from the repository secrets.

Data retention and deletion

The refresh token is kept only as long as the App is in use. It is deleted when access is revoked or the App is discontinued.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
